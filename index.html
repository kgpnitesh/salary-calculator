Assumptions & Notes
  • Basic Pay as on 01.07.2028 = current Basic Pay moved down two cells (two annual increments) in the same 7th CPC pay level, per the Pay Matrix. If fewer than two cells remain, pay stagnates at the last cell of the level (noted on the slip).
  • Basic Pay after 8th CPC = (Basic Pay as on 01.07.2028) × Fitment Factor, rounded to the nearest rupee.
  • DA = 0 after the 8th CPC, so Gross Pay = Basic Pay after 8th CPC + HRA + TA.
  • HRA at 24% / 16% / 8% (X / Y / Z) of Basic Pay after 8th CPC.
  • Transport Allowance = 7th CPC base rate × Fitment Factor (the "+DA" component is nil): Level 9 and above: ₹7,200 (X) / ₹3,600 (Y, Z); Levels 3–8: ₹3,600 / ₹1,800; Levels 1–2: ₹3,600 / ₹1,800 where pay ≥ ₹24,200, else ₹1,350 / ₹900. The ₹24,200 threshold is tested against the 7th CPC Basic Pay as on 01.07.2028, since the rate table is a 7th CPC table. The special ₹15,750 rate (Level 14+, staff car admissible but not availed) and double TA for PwD employees are not modelled.
  • Income tax on salary (Gross Pay × 12) under the new tax regime slabs currently in force (FY 2025-26): ₹0–4L nil; 4–8L @5%; 8–12L @10%; 12–16L @15%; 16–20L @20%; 20–24L @25%; above 24L @30%. Standard deduction of ₹75,000 is allowed; full rebate u/s 87A where taxable income ≤ ₹12,00,000, with marginal relief just above it; Health & Education Cess @4%. Slabs for FY 2028-29 are not yet notified — update here if they change.
  • CGHS: ₹250 (Levels 1–5), ₹450 (Level 6), ₹650 (Levels 7–11), ₹1,000 (Level 12 and above, including 13, 13A, 14–16).
  • Employee's own NPS contribution (80CCD(1)) is not deductible under the new regime, so it does not reduce taxable income. CGHS is also not deducted from taxable income under the new regime.
  • Pay Matrix cells for Levels 1–13A are as per the Gazette table supplied; Levels 14–16 are generated by the standard matrix rule (3% increment, rounded to nearest ₹100) — verify the top cells of Levels 14–16 against the Gazette before relying on them.
  • Other deductions (CGEGIS, licence fee, professional tax, recoveries) are not modelled.
